Michigan State Spartans NIL valuations

97 athletes · Big Ten · estimated ranges as of August 2026

Michigan State holds full Big Ten membership and the media revenue that comes with it, but its football NIL market is smaller than those of the league's headline programs. Very few Spartan players show up in national NIL valuation databases, so most published player figures are estimates built from role and position rather than reported agreements. The program's clearest financial lever is the House settlement: since 2025 the school can share revenue directly with athletes under a cap of roughly $20.5 million in year one that rises each season, a mechanism that does not depend on collective fundraising. Basketball has traditionally been the stronger national brand in East Lansing, which shapes how NIL attention is distributed.
