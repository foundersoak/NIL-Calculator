Oregon State Beavers NIL valuations

1 athletes · WCC · estimated ranges as of August 2026

This entry covers Oregon State gymnastics, a program inside an athletic department reshaped by the Pac-12's 2024 collapse, after which Oregon State placed sports with new conference partners while working to rebuild the Pac-12. Gymnastics NIL bears little resemblance to football: earnings come mainly from individual social media followings, sponsorships, and camps rather than donor collectives, budgets are a small fraction of football spending, and figures are almost never disclosed. Oregon State's reduced media revenue after realignment also limits what it can offer under the House settlement, which since 2025 allows direct revenue sharing up to a cap of roughly $20.5 million that few schools outside the power conferences approach. Top gymnasts with large followings can still out-earn athletes in bigger sports.
