NIL at Virginia Cavaliers

Virginia's NIL structure runs through Cav Futures, the collective founded in 2023 to organize donor support for Cavaliers athletes, with football and a historically strong basketball program as its main clients. The program's public NIL footprint is small: a 2026 review of the football roster found no published player level valuations, so figures for Virginia athletes are modeled rather than reported. The ACC provides power conference standing and media revenue, and the 2025 House settlement lets the school pay athletes directly, about 20.5 million dollars across sports in year one with the cap rising annually. Reporting around Virginia's recent transfer portal classes described increased roster investment, though the school discloses little about how money is allocated.
