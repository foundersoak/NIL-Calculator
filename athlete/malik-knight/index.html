NIL at Pittsburgh Panthers

Pitt's NIL effort is anchored by Alliance 412, the collective serving Panther athletes, in a city where college programs share attention with the Steelers, Penguins, and Pirates. ACC membership still provides the revenue base for House settlement participation: since 2025 Pitt can pay athletes directly under the first-year cap of about $20.5 million, which rises annually, a mechanism that matters most for programs without deep collective fundraising.
