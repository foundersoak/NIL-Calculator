NIL at Missouri Tigers

Missouri benefits from one of the most permissive state NIL laws in the country, which allowed direct school involvement in deals earlier than most states, and its NIL operation runs through Every True Tiger, a marketing agency model rather than a traditional donor collective. As an SEC member, Missouri has the revenue to fund House settlement payments to athletes, capped around $20.5 million departmentwide in year one and rising annually, layered under third party agreements. Football commands most of the spending. The St. Louis and Kansas City business corridors give marketable Tigers endorsement options beyond mid-Missouri, though relatively few players appear in national NIL valuation rankings compared with the conference's biggest brands.
