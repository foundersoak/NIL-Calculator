NIL at Ole Miss Rebels

Ole Miss moved early and aggressively in the NIL era, with the Grove Collective becoming one of the SEC's most active fundraising operations and the football program leaning heavily on the transfer portal to build rosters. That posture made the Rebels competitive with far larger athletic budgets. Since the 2025 House settlement, the school can also pay athletes directly through revenue sharing, up to roughly $20.5 million in year one with annual growth, and SEC media money makes full funding feasible.
