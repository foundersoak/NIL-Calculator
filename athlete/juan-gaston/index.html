NIL at Georgia Bulldogs

Georgia restructured its NIL operation as the revenue-sharing era began. Its original donor collective, the Classic City Collective, shut down, and in June 2025 the athletic association partnered with Learfield Impact to run a unified in-house NIL agency for Bulldog athletes, later adding a third-party initiative called Glory Glory. Under the House settlement Georgia shares revenue directly with athletes, subject to the cap of about $20.5 million in year one that rises annually, and football, a two-time national champion earlier this decade, receives the largest allocation. Georgia's enormous fanbase and Atlanta's corporate market support substantial third-party deals on top of school payments, though the program discloses few individual figures.
