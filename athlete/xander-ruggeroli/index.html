NIL at Arizona State Sun Devils

Arizona State plays football in the Big 12, and its NIL market expanded noticeably after the program won the 2024 conference title and reached the College Football Playoff. Donor-driven NIL activity has centered on the Sun Angel Collective, while the Phoenix metro area gives athletes one of the larger local endorsement markets in the conference. Like other power-conference schools, Arizona State can now pay athletes directly under the House settlement, with a school-wide cap of about $20.5 million in the first year that increases over time, and third-party deals continue on top of that. Public valuation data for individual Sun Devils remains limited, so most published figures for the roster are estimates rather than reported contracts.
