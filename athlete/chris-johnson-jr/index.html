NIL at Clemson Tigers

Clemson's NIL economy is built on one of the most established donor cultures in college football, anchored by the IPTAY booster organization founded in 1934 and supplemented by collectives such as Dear Old Clemson. Public valuation data for Clemson players is unusually deep; a 2026 roster review produced researched figures for roughly twice as many Tigers as at a typical program.
