NIL at Syracuse Orange

Syracuse sits in the middle of the ACC's NIL economy, with football and a storied basketball program drawing from the same donor pool. The football side has pushed to raise its profile since Fran Brown took over ahead of the 2024 season, using the transfer portal more aggressively than past staffs, while basketball remains the school's traditional identity in central New York.
