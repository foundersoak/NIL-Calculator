NIL at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets

Georgia Tech's NIL market benefits from its location in Atlanta, one of the largest corporate sponsorship markets in the South, though the program competes for attention with professional franchises and with Georgia's statewide fanbase. The Yellow Jackets have been a mid-tier ACC spender, with donor NIL activity smaller than at the conference's football-first powers. Since the 2025 House settlement, Georgia Tech can pay athletes directly under the standard cap of roughly $20.5 million in the first year, growing annually, and third-party deals continue separately.
