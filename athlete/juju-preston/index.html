NIL at Clemson Tigers

Clemson's NIL economy is built on one of the most established donor cultures in college football, anchored by the IPTAY booster organization founded in 1934 and supplemented by collectives such as Dear Old Clemson. Public valuation data for Clemson players is unusually deep; a 2026 roster review produced researched figures for roughly twice as many Tigers as at a typical program. Clemson also settled its legal dispute with the ACC in 2025, securing a media-revenue formula that rewards its television draw and strengthens the budget behind revenue sharing. Under the House settlement the school can pay athletes directly, up to about $20.5 million department-wide in year one and more each season after, with football taking the leading share.
