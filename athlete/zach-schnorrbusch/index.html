NIL at Boston College Eagles

Boston College fields one of the smaller NIL operations among ACC football programs. It is a private Jesuit school in a metropolitan area dominated by professional franchises, which limits local endorsement demand for college athletes, and its donor NIL infrastructure has been modest compared with Southern peers. The program still benefits from ACC membership and full power-conference media revenue, and like nearly every power-league school it can share revenue directly with athletes under the House settlement, capped near $20.5 million department-wide in year one and rising after that. Third-party deals continue alongside those payments. Public NIL valuations for individual Eagles are rare, so most figures for the roster are modeled estimates rather than disclosed deals.
