How much does Orion Irving make in NIL?

Offensive Lineman · Vanderbilt Commodores · SEC

Irving is a senior offensive lineman from Houston who joined Vanderbilt in 2025 after three seasons at Sam Houston. He appeared in eight games in his first Commodores season, taking 108 snaps at both right and left tackle. He captained the offensive line at Lamar High School.

NIL at Vanderbilt Commodores

Vanderbilt is the SEC's smallest school and its NIL market has historically trailed the league, but that picture is changing. The notable exception is quarterback Jared Curtis, a true freshman whose reported compensation of around 2 million dollars was among the largest ever attached to a player yet to take a college snap, a sign of new donor willingness in Nashville. Under the 2025 House settlement, Vanderbilt can share revenue directly with athletes, up to roughly 20.5 million dollars in year one and rising, the same cap as every SEC rival despite its smaller fanbase.
